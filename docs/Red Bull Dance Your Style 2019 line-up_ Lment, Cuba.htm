Meet Lment, the winner of Red Bull Dance Your Style Cuba
Lment is a jack of all trades and is known for breaking over everything from rumba to classical music. Find out why he was destined to dance and what he'll bring to Red Bull Dance Your Style in Paris.
Written by Tracy Kawalik
2 min readPublished on 08.10.2019 · 05:24 UTC-3
Immersed in the soulful rhythms and rich musical history of his birthplace, Cuban dancer Lment was in many ways destined to dance.
“I became a dancer because I was inspired by Micheal Jackson,” he says. Swerving over multiple styles that range from Cuban rumba to breaking, power moves and acrobatics - Lment excitedly explains that he would like to fuse classical and krump ballet into his street dance flow next.
Lment started studying ballet at the age of 18 and went on to graduate from Cuba’s National School of Art. Today he trains every day as a member of the Ballet of Cuban Televicion.
“I can't imagine being anything other than a dancer or dreaming of being anywhere except in the world of dance,” he says. “The music is so important – it’s the motivator of so many of my movements.”
When asked what he would play if he could get on the decks and select his own music at Dance Your Style, Lment replies, “Cuban rumba or Afrobeat” before breaking down the moment he locked down a place in the finals.

“Winning a spot in the Dance Your Style World Final was an unforgettable experience," he says. "All the dancers that will battle are excellent. So for Paris, I’m training every day to have another specific element of my dance prepared and perfected.
“In a battle, I focus on enjoying it and living the experience. As a dancer, you have to enjoy everything that you do on the scene and believe in yourself. Those are the most important skills to master” Lment concludes as he makes his way back into the studio.
